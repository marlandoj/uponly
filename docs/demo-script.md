# Demo video script (~2 minutes)

Hackyard Yard #4 requires a demo video of the real mundane task getting done.
Shoot on two phones; screen-record both.

## Shot list

1. **Hook (0:00–0:15)** — "Chores are invisible work. ChoreQuest makes them legendary."
   Show the home screen: circle name, boss battle bar, members roster.

2. **The mundane task (0:15–0:45)** — Phone A: start a real quest
   (e.g. "Clean the kitchen sink"), take the before photo with the camera.
   Narrate the 4-minute minimum timer (time-lapse or cut).

3. **Completion (0:45–1:05)** — After photo, complete. Show +10 XP, the boss
   taking damage ("You defeated the Grease Goblin!"), streak flame appearing.

4. **Celebration (1:05–1:35)** — Tap "Share for celebration": QR code fills
   the screen. Phone B scans it — before/after photos appear, B taps
   "Legendary 🏆". Cut to A: level ticks up, "First Fold badge unlocked!
   soulbound (simulated)".

5. **Leaderboard (1:35–1:50)** — /leaderboard: medals, levels, streaks.
   "Positive-only — ratings never subtract."

6. **Close (1:50–2:00)** — "ChoreQuest: real chores, epic loot."
   Show the repo URL.

## Notes for the edit
- Blur or skip the magic-link email step; start both phones logged in.
- The "simulated" label on the mint is intentional — call it out as the
  pre-go/no-go state, not a bug.
- Keep the mundane task genuinely mundane. That's the point.
